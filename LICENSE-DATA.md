The files under `datasets/` and `results/` that we authored are licensed under the
**Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0)**,
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>. This covers the contract library, the trap

recorded system outputs.

The paper under `paper/` is published by ACM under CC BY 4.0 as well.

**Upstream content.** Many of these files embed fields taken from FinanceReasoning
(Tang et al., ACL 2025): questions, contexts, reference functions, solutions and gold answers.
FinanceReasoning's repository carries no license file, so those fields are not ours to license
and remain subject to the upstream terms. We do not redistribute the FinanceReasoning source
files themselves; `datasets/README.md` explains how to fetch them at the commit we used.
